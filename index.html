Welcome

Hi, I am Dr. Pablo Merchán-Rivera, an AI engineer based in Munich.

I am originally from Ecuador and hold a doctoral degree in engineering from the Technical University of Munich (2022). Over the last three years, my main focus has been AI engineering: building reliable LLM applications, evaluation workflows, and production-ready ML systems.

My background in numerical modeling, uncertainty quantification, and Bayesian methods continues to shape how I design practical AI solutions for real-world problems.
