Building a RAG Pipeline with MongoDB: Vector Search for Personalized Picks

Aug 1, 2026

This article explores the construction of recommendations system using a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipeline. The objective is to learn how to harness the power of MongoDB's vector search capabilities, transform data descriptions into searchable digital fingerprints, and create a system that understands the nuances of your preferences and your communication. I show this with one example: a movie recommendation system that's not just smart, but also efficient.
